## 🚀 Conversion (CRO) improvements I added
All tuned to stay quiet and premium — no loud "cheap webinar" tactics.

**Trust & credibility (big for a supplement purchase)**
- Slim **announcement bar**: free shipping over $75 · third-party lab tested · 30-day guarantee · ethically sourced.
- **Trust strip** under the hero: Third-Party Lab Tested · Vegan & Non-GMO · Ethically Sourced · Adaptogen Powered.
- **Star rating + social proof** in the hero ("4.9/5 from 2,000+ focused minds").

**Reduce risk & friction**
- **Risk-reversal band**: "Try it for 30 days, risk-free." Removing risk is one of the strongest conversion levers.
- **FAQ** rewritten to answer the real hesitations a first-time buyer has.

**Always-available path to buy**
- A persistent **"Shop Now"** button in the header.
- A **sticky "Shop Organic Blends" bar on mobile** that follows the user — most traffic is mobile.
- **5-star ratings + clear "Shop Now"** on each product card.

**Social proof & engagement**
- Testimonials as a **rotating carousel** with named "Verified Buyer" reviewers.
- Newsletter shows an **instant "You're in" confirmation** and states the value (discounts, workshops, rituals).
