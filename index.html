Frequently Asked Questions

How do these daily blends work?

Our formulas use small, daily servings of functional mushrooms and adaptogens to help support focus, mood, and resilience as part of a consistent routine. They're crafted for people who want a clear, grounded edge in their work and life. If you're pregnant, nursing, or on medication, check with your physician first.

How do I take Illuminate Mind blends?

Most people take one capsule with water in the morning, with or without food. Start with a single serving for the first week to feel how your body responds, then keep a consistent daily ritual. Many follow a 5-days-on, 2-days-off rhythm.

Are your products third-party tested?

Always. Every batch is vetted under high-end clinical lab standards, and full Certificates of Analysis (COAs) are accessible via a QR code on each bottle. We use fruiting bodies only, never cheap mycelium-on-grain or starch fillers.

What's the difference between Andean Heart and the Balance Blend?

Andean Heart is a focused botanical blend with L-theanine, built for clear, lifted focus. The Matias Romero Balance Blend leans toward calm, grounded balance for a steady, centered day. Many alternate the two.

How long until I feel results, and what's your guarantee?

Many notice a calmer focus within the first week or two; the supportive benefits build with consistent daily use over 3–4 weeks. Give it an honest 30 days. If you don't love it, we'll refund every cent.
