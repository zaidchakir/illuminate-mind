Frequently Asked Questions

What is microdosing, and is it right for me?

Microdosing means taking small, sub-perceptual amounts of functional botanicals and adaptogens daily to support focus, mood, and resilience — without intoxication. Our blends are crafted for people who want a clear, grounded edge in their work and life. If you're pregnant, nursing, or on medication, check with your physician first.

How do I take Illuminate Mind blends?

Most people take one capsule with water in the morning, with or without food. Start with a single serving for the first week to feel how your body responds, then keep a consistent daily ritual. Many follow a 5-days-on, 2-days-off rhythm.

Are your products third-party tested?

Always. Every batch is vetted under high-end clinical lab standards, and full Certificates of Analysis (COAs) are accessible via a QR code on each bottle. We use fruiting bodies only — never cheap mycelium-on-grain or starch fillers.

What's the difference between Andean Heart and the Balance Blend?

Andean Heart is a single-plant formula built for presence, connection, and expansive focus. The Matias Romero Balance Blend leans toward calm, grounded balance — pairing ceremonial botanicals with L-theanine for steady, lifted clarity. Many alternate the two.

How long until I feel results — and what's your guarantee?

Some feel a calmer focus within days; the deeper neuroplastic benefits build over 3–4 weeks of consistent use. Give it an honest 30 days — if you don't love it, we'll refund every cent.
